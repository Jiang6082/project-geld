# Paper-execution, metric and candidate-evidence review — November 14, 2026

Reviewed from `3d13e41`. Like the [September audit](AUDIT_2026-09-13.md), this
review corrects how evidence is produced and how paper orders are planned. It
does not establish a strategy's profitability, and historical research reports
were not rewritten.

## Implemented fixes

| Priority | Finding | Resulting behavior |
|---|---|---|
| High | Daily V4 / Momentum V2-V3 counted their rebalance schedule from the first bar of the data. Paper planning fetches a rolling 800-day window whose first bar moves daily, so the sleeve submitted on a rebalance day could be a selection made up to 20 sessions earlier, on a drifting phase. On synthetic data, shifting the window start by 3-12 sessions changed the final-day sleeve. | Strategies take `rebalance_anchor` (`"start"`, the backtest default, or `"end"`). `run_paper_cycle` anchors at the latest bar, so every paper rebalance is a fresh selection at the decision bar and no longer depends on the window start. Backtests are unchanged. |
| High | Positions whose stock left the universe were reported as unmanaged, never sold, and permanently reduced available gross exposure. Intra V15's refresh refused such changes; Daily V4's universe export did not. | A per-strategy ledger (`managed_symbols-<prefix>.json`) records every symbol the strategy holds or trades. Ledger symbols outside the universe are fetched, priced and sold at the next submitting rebalance, but never offered to the strategy as candidates. A ledger name with no bars stays owned and unmanaged, so it still counts against gross exposure. Remaining unmanaged positions trigger a warning; `geld adopt-positions --symbols ...` (or `--all` on a dedicated account) adopts ones stranded before the ledger existed. |
| High | The documentation called the Intra V15 shortfall kill-switch fail-safe, but its log read was unguarded and the log was written non-atomically; an empty or truncated file aborted the cycle before planning. | The log is written atomically. An unreadable log is renamed `.corrupt-<timestamp>`, a warning is printed, and the cycle continues. If the switch cannot be evaluated, only the base sleeve sits out that cycle; monitoring never aborts trading. |
| High | Shortfall was measured seconds after submission. Marketable limit orders that filled later stayed `missed` forever and were excluded from the trailing mean, so the 2 bps gate only saw instant fills. | Each submitting cycle re-reads broker activity back to the oldest unresolved order (up to five days) and re-scores those rows. A re-scoring failure no longer drops the current cycle's rows. |
| Medium | The kill-switch only "latched" because a flat sleeve stops producing fills; its own flattening fill, or a rewritten log, could re-enable it, and there was no reset path. | A breach is persisted in `kill_switch_latch.json` and holds until `geld reset-kill-switch`, which also stops earlier rows from counting. The latched message names the command. |
| Medium | Orders were budgeted and submitted alphabetically: buys could be sent before the sales that funded them, and new longs could exhaust the budget before cover-short buys, which are always kept. | Orders are budgeted and submitted risk-reducing first, then sells before buys; symbols stay alphabetical within each group. |
| Medium | Sortino used the standard deviation of clipped returns, overstating it by about 20% on a typical series. | Downside deviation is the root-mean-square of negative returns against a 0% target. |
| Medium | Every metric counted the opening valuation as a 0% return, and intraday daily metrics dropped the first session's return. | The opening row is a balance, not a return. Intraday daily metrics start from the opening valuation (without duplicating it when the first session has a single bar). |
| Medium | The candidate capacity gate used full-period turnover, diluted by warmup periods with no positions. | `turnover_capacity` judges holdout turnover; full-period turnover is still reported. |
| Medium | `client_order_id` truncated strategy names to eight characters, so `intra_v13` and `intra_v15` both became `intra_v1`. | Underscores are removed and long names keep their first and last four characters (`intrav15`, `dailyv4`). A test checks that tokens are unique across the registry. |
| Medium | Intra V15 inherits from V13 -> V12 -> V8 -> V7 and Daily V4 wraps Momentum V3; an edit to a research-only parent could silently change a paper strategy. | `tests/test_golden_targets.py` freezes the target-weight changes of the deployed TOML parameters on fixed synthetic data, for both the backtest and the paper (latest-bar) schedules. Mutating V7's dislocation rule or V3's exit buffer fails it. |
| High | Candidate re-validation had no correction for how many variants were searched before the candidate, and no check that Geld's holdout followed the discovery data. | Optional bundle fields `research_trials` and `discovery_window`. A `holdout_after_discovery` gate applies when a window is declared (a date-only end covers the whole day); otherwise the verdict and validator warn. A `deflated_sharpe` gate (Bailey & López de Prado, threshold 0.95) counts every evaluation on the holdout, plus the discovery search unless the holdout is provably after it. In batches the trial-Sharpe variance is floored at each candidate's sampling variance, so near-duplicate candidates cannot lower the bar. The implementation reproduces the paper's worked example (DSR 0.9004). |

## Compatibility and interpretation

- **Daily V4 paper targets change.** On the next rebalance the submitted sleeve
  is a fresh selection at the latest bar. Expect a one-time difference from what
  the previous phase would have chosen.
- **Order IDs change format.** Deploy outside market hours. A cycle retried
  across the upgrade uses new IDs; open orders are still skipped by symbol and
  filled orders are reflected in positions, so this does not duplicate orders.
- **Reported metrics shift slightly.** Sharpe, volatility and alpha lose the
  spurious first zero; Sortino falls to its standard definition; intraday total
  return includes the first session. Earlier research numbers were not
  recomputed because their raw datasets are not in the repository.
- **Candidate verdicts can become stricter.** Batch promotion now also needs the
  deflated Sharpe, and holdout turnover is usually higher than diluted
  full-period turnover. Bundles that declare an overlapping `discovery_window`
  are rejected. Declaring a `discovery_window` that precedes the holdout is
  rewarded: the discovery search then no longer counts toward the deflated
  Sharpe's trials.
- **`--trials` means evaluations on this holdout.** Pass the running count of
  candidates you have tested against the same holdout, so repeated single runs
  are deflated like a batch.
- **Emberforge follow-up.** Export `discovery_window` and `research_trials` in
  `geld_bundles`; until then, bundles stay valid with warnings.

## Not addressed here

1. The ownership ledger covers `paper-once` and `daily-close-check`. The
   intraday path keeps relying on the universe-refresh guard, because its
   positions are flat by 15:45.
2. Sells are submitted first but the planner does not wait for their fills
   before buying. Accounts with tight buying power can still see a rejected buy.
3. Geld does not yet keep its own count of evaluations per holdout, so
   `--trials` relies on the caller. The deflated Sharpe and batch p-values also
   assume roughly independent observations; dependence-aware calibration (HAC
   or block bootstrap) remains open from the September audit.
4. Splitting the large CLI and paper modules and replacing `print` with
   structured logging remain open.

## Validation

The full suite passes (272 passed, 14 skipped: the skips are optional
Emberforge cross-repository and environment-dependent tests). The metric
regressions fail on `3d13e41`, the rebalance-anchor test reproduces the phase
dependence with the old schedule, and the golden tests fail when V7's
dislocation rule or V3's exit buffer is mutated. A second, independent review
of the diff (including an end-to-end `paper-once` run with a fake broker and
data source) found the latch, budget-order, variance-floor, date-boundary,
adoption and single-bar issues fixed above. No Alpaca account or order was
used.
