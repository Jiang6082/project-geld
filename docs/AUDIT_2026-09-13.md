# Engineering and research-integrity review — September 13, 2026

Reviewed from `bece5c4`. This review prioritizes correctness of evidence,
candidate boundaries, durable state, interoperability, and reproducible checks.
It does not establish a strategy's profitability. Existing research reports and
paper-account configuration are historical records and were not rewritten.

## Compatibility and interpretation

- Rejected imports must use a new candidate ID for a revised hypothesis. An
  import never resets the existing record; copying a file over it would defeat
  the audit trail.
- `--max-symbols` is a deterministic diagnostic cap, not a liquidity ranking.
  Supply explicit causal membership data for a PIT liquidity study. The legacy
  `research_broad_pit` binding source label is retained for compatibility and is
  not proof of the dataset's construction.
- New validation may reject a bundle that used to run. Correct the recorded
  specification/evidence and assign a new identity rather than weakening gates.
- Statistical and accounting corrections can change candidate verdicts and
  historical metrics. Previously published strategy numbers are not recomputed
  here because their raw market datasets are not included in the repository.
- The existing independent candidate strategy defaults to long-only. An
  Emberforge long/short diagnostic is not an equivalent executable portfolio;
  record and evaluate the chosen construction explicitly.

## Remaining research and operational work

These are outstanding limitations, not implemented capabilities:

1. **Prospective, fixed-date evidence.** Register a fixed hypothesis, universe,
   untouched date range and research budget before testing. This review repairs
   batch multiplicity; repeated batches or adapting strategies after viewing a
   holdout still require a persistent experiment/holdout protocol.
2. **Dependence-aware statistics.** The Sharpe p-value is an IID normal
   approximation. Serial correlation, heteroskedasticity and dependent candidate
   tests need HAC or block-bootstrap calibration and, where appropriate, a more
   conservative dependence-aware multiple-testing procedure. BH is not a
   universal false-discovery guarantee under arbitrary dependence.
3. **Historical data quality.** Replay the corrected engine on versioned
   point-in-time membership, corporate actions, delistings and independently
   sourced benchmark data. Merely observing bars does not prove that a stock
   would have been eligible at the time.
4. **Execution and capacity evidence.** Use actual paper fill logs and historical
   borrow/locate information to calibrate spread, impact, short availability,
   financing and partial fills. No external account or order was used in this
   review, so production recovery and fill behavior remain unverified here.
5. **Process ownership and durable writes.** Exclusive candidate imports are
   protected. Other read/modify/write ledgers and paper loops would benefit from
   a single-writer process lock, transactional event journal, recovery tests and
   tested backups before concurrent execution is supported.
6. **Maintainability.** Separate the large CLI/paper modules into focused
   commands and services; move repeated strategy infrastructure into shared
   helpers only with target/fill equivalence fixtures. Do not merge distinct
   research variants merely because their code looks similar.
7. **Reproducible environments and data.** CI validates supported Python/OS
   combinations against declared dependency ranges. A release-level dependency
   lock and content-addressed data snapshots remain useful for long-lived
   research reproduction.

Emberforge operator parity. `python -m build` validates wheel/source packaging.

request; synthetic checks do not substitute for market-data revalidation.
